Safe & Complete

Removing dead, dying or unwanted trees — safely

A dead or damaged tree can be more than an eyesore — it can be a serious risk to your home, family and property. Our crew handles tree removals of every size across Green Ridge and Sedalia, using the right equipment and technique for tight yards, tall trees and everything in between.

Service 02

Tree Trimming

Precision Pruning

Trimming that keeps trees healthy and shapely

Regular trimming does more than keep your trees looking their best — it removes weak or crossing branches, improves airflow and light, and keeps limbs safely clear of your roof, power lines and walkways.

Our crew prunes with the long-term health of the tree in mind, using clean cuts placed to encourage strong future growth rather than just hacking back branches.

What's included

  • ✓ Deadwood and hazard-branch removal
  • ✓ Canopy shaping and thinning
  • ✓ Clearance trimming around roofs & power lines
  • ✓ Branch and clipping clean-up included
  • ✓ Recommendations for ongoing tree health
①

Inspection

We evaluate each tree's structure and health before making a single cut.

②

Plan the Cuts

③

Trim & Shape

Clean, precise pruning that supports the tree's natural form.

④
